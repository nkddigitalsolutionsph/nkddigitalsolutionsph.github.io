Our framework

A structured approach to digital growth.

The NKD Growth Framework

  1. 01

    AUDIT

    Review website structure, content, search visibility, existing workflows and potential gaps.

  2. 02

    RESEARCH

    Research keywords, search intent, audience needs, competitors and industry context.

  3. 03

    STRATEGY

    Build a practical plan that connects business objectives with website, content and SEO priorities.

  4. 04

    OPTIMIZATION

    Improve website pages, metadata, structure, publishing processes and content quality.

  5. 05

    CONTENT

    Develop useful, researched content and manage publishing and quality checks.

  6. 06

    MEASUREMENT

    Monitor available performance data, document observations and recommend next steps.
