How big the forex market is

Figures from the Bank for International Settlements Triennial Central Bank Survey, April 2025.

$9.6 trnAverage daily trading in foreign exchange markets.Up 28% from $7.5 trn in April 2022.
31%Share of daily turnover that is spot FX, about $3.0 trn a day.Outright forwards were 19%.
89.2%Share of trades with the US dollar on one side.Euro 28.9%, yen 16.8%, sterling 10.2%.

Source: BIS, OTC foreign exchange turnover in April 2025. Currency shares add up to 200% because every trade has two currencies.

The risk side of the numbers

Regulators have measured how retail traders actually do with leveraged products.

74% to 89%Of retail CFD accounts typically lose money, across EU authorities studied.ESMA, 2018 analysis.
82%Of clients in the FCA sample of retail CFD accounts lost money.FCA, 2018 analysis.

These are loss rates for leveraged CFD accounts, which include forex CFDs. They describe past samples and do not predict any one person's result.
